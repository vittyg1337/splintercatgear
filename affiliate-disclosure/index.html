How Splintercat may make money

Clear links. No hidden loyalties.

Splintercat may earn money in the future when a reader buys through a clearly labelled link. This page explains the rules and the site’s current status.

The short version

If you buy through an affiliate link on Splintercat, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. A disclosure will appear before the first affiliate link on every monetized page.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link contains tracking information that allows a retailer or network to attribute a qualifying purchase to Splintercat. The retailer may then pay us a portion of the sale or another defined fee.

What affiliate money cannot buy

It cannot buy a ranking, rating, recommendation, test outcome, or favourable edit. Retailer commission rates may differ, but suitability and evidence determine the editorial conclusion.

How links will be identified

Monetized pages will display a plain-language disclosure close to the relevant recommendation and before the first affiliate link. A paid-link label will appear beside each monetized retailer button, and affiliate links will use appropriate machine-readable attributes such as rel="sponsored".

Amazon Associates

If Amazon Associates tracking is activated, every page carrying those links will display Amazon’s required statement clearly before the first link: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” Splintercat will never invent a tracking ID or describe an ordinary retailer link as monetized.

Prices and availability

Retail prices, stock, promotions, shipping, taxes, warranties, and return policies can change without notice. A link is not a guarantee of the price or availability shown when an article was last checked. Canadian and U.S. retailer options will be distinguished where practical.

Free samples, discounts, and sponsorships

If an item was supplied, discounted, loaned, or connected to a sponsorship, that fact will be disclosed on the relevant page. Sponsored content, if ever accepted, will be clearly labelled and kept distinct from independent editorial rankings.

The current launch state

The popular-gear watchlist currently uses ordinary Amazon.com links and earns no commission. The indoor-cat setup guide also contains no affiliate links. This disclosure and every affected page will be updated before monetized tracking begins.
