# Splintercat 12-Month Organic Content Calendar

**Period:** September 2026–August 2027

**Cadence:** Two substantive deliverables per month

**Default label:** Research Guide unless the evidence genuinely supports another label

**Operating rule:** Quality and verification outrank cadence. If source review or HTTPS/indexing QA is incomplete, move the publication date rather than publishing a thin page.

## Calendar objectives

- Establish topical depth around habitat, litter, feeding/hydration, enrichment, and travel.
- Prioritize specific household constraints over generic “best product” queries.
- Create natural paths from setup guidance to buying checks without making purchase links the substance.
- Develop reusable original assets: room maps, measurement sheets, maintenance logs, and total-cost worksheets.
- Earn enough verified query data to reprioritize the second half of the year.

Search volumes are deliberately absent. Priority is based on intent fit, the present information architecture, evidence requirements, and the opportunity to be more useful than a generic roundup. Re-score the next quarter after each complete 28-day Search Console period.

## Quarterly gates

### Before the October publishing cycle

- HTTPS resolves correctly and is enforced.
- Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools are verified and the sitemap is submitted.
- Every existing guide is in the sitemap and reachable from `/guides/`.
- A source-log and pre-publication checklist are in use.

### Before the January commercial guide

- Current manuals and specifications are archived in the working research record.
- The page label, evidence limits, affiliate status, and retailer-link treatment are approved.
- Analytics can distinguish informational navigation from retailer outbound clicks.
- The guide contains enough original decision support to remain useful without clicking a retailer.

### Before the April and May equipment guides

- A category method exists for any measured claim.
- Products are called “tested” only when complete hands-on records and original evidence exist.
- Safety and health-adjacent language receives a second editorial review.
- Product model, version, research date, and current availability context are visible.

### Before the annual pillar update

- Export 12 months of query/page data by cluster, device, and country.
- Identify cannibalization, pages with impressions in positions 8–30, and pages losing clicks after stable demand.
- Review whether the two-item cadence produced quality; reduce it if sources, testing, or maintenance lagged.
- Decide the next year’s calendar from verified gaps rather than repeating the first-year assumptions.

## Required content brief

Create a brief before drafting. It must include:

1. Primary query, search intent, URL owner, and the pages it must not duplicate.
2. Reader scenario and constraints: space, cat/household count, cleaning, noise, accessibility, budget, location.
3. Direct answer and decision framework.
4. Claims that need evidence, preferred primary/professional sources, and access dates.
5. Original artifact to create: diagram, worksheet, measurements, maintenance log, or test evidence.
6. Required internal links and the pages that will link back after publication.
7. Evidence label, byline, reviewer need, veterinary caveat, and known limitations.
8. Commercial next step, if any, and affiliate compliance status.
9. Metadata, image, schema, accessibility, and crawl QA requirements.
10. Expected review cadence and triggers for an early update.

## Production workflow

1. **Search review:** inspect current result types and professional sources; do not copy competitor structure.
2. **Brief approval:** resolve page ownership and evidence needs before drafting.
3. **Research:** keep a claim/source log and archive current manuals where permitted.
4. **Draft:** answer the decision early, include drawbacks and skip criteria, and avoid filler.
5. **Evidence review:** verify each consequential claim against its source and qualify uncertainty.
6. **Editorial review:** check byline, label, disclosure, limitations, dates, and correction path.
7. **Build:** add metadata, owned imagery, structured data, internal links, and sitemap entry.
8. **QA:** validate HTML/JSON-LD, links, accessibility, mobile layout, and page performance.
9. **Publish and inspect:** request indexing for priority pages only after live HTTPS and canonical checks pass.
10. **Learn:** review queries after enough data accumulates; improve the answer rather than mechanically adding keywords.

## Refresh queue rules

Move a page into the refresh queue when any of these is true:

- Its material source, regulation, product version, or manufacturer instruction changes.
- A retailer destination is broken, redirected unexpectedly, or no longer represents the described item.
- Search Console shows a persistent mismatch between query intent and landing-page answer.
- Two pages compete for the same query without distinct reader needs.
- A page gains substantial impressions in positions 8–30 and can be improved with missing evidence or clearer decision support.
- A factual error affects the recommendation or safety context.
- The scheduled review interval has arrived.

Do not refresh solely to change a date, add synonyms, or preserve a promised publishing count.

## Deferred topics

Broad category guides for fountains, litter boxes, and carriers remain in the keyword backlog. Toy, scratching-post, and small-space cat-tree Research Shortlists are live and should expand only when another exact record adds a genuinely different use case. Single-product hands-on reviews remain deferred until the team has the physical item, a documented method, original evidence, and enough comparison context to make a useful judgment.
