Buggin’ Out Puzzle & Play

The short answer: A paw-operated puzzle that turns part of a meal or treat allowance into a foraging task.

By Splintercat Editorial TeamNot hands-on testedSources checked August 30, 2026Methodology

Product evidence record

What this format is designed to do

Sliding leaves and movable pegs cover multiple food cups, offering a more deliberate problem-solving activity than a free-moving toy.

Play style
Foraging puzzle
Size (L × W × H)
13.78 × 11.42 × 1.77 in
Capacity
About 1/4 cup
Care
Hand wash
May suit

Food-motivated cats ready for a beginner-to-intermediate puzzle and households willing to teach the mechanism gradually.

Skip if

Your cat guards food around other animals, chews the board, needs a veterinary feeding plan that excludes puzzle use, or becomes distressed by food frustration.

Check before buying

  • Confirm the exact model and current food-contact care instructions.
  • Count puzzle food within the cat’s normal daily intake.
  • For multiple cats, decide whether each needs a separated puzzle station.

Safety and failure checks

  • Supervise initial sessions and begin with compartments partly exposed.
  • Remove the puzzle if the cat chews or damages the board or moving pieces.
  • Wash after wet-food use and discard food left out beyond your veterinarian’s or food maker’s guidance.

Maintenance burden

Hand wash with mild soap, rinse thoroughly, dry fully, and inspect the integrated moving pieces before refilling.
