What this format is designed to do

Its published dimensions make it useful as a marketplace comparison point against the shorter SmartCat post, especially for long-bodied vertical scratchers.

May suit

Cats that stretch high, buyers comparing tall posts, and homes with a clear square floor area near the existing scratch target.

Skip if

A dangling toy encourages chewing, the base would obstruct circulation, or your cat has shown a strong preference for cardboard or horizontal scratching.

Check before buying

  • Verify the exact large 35.4-inch variant before ordering.
  • Confirm the latest assembly instructions and listed surface material.
  • Check whether the hanging toy can be removed if your cat chews cords or small parts.

Safety and failure checks

  • Inspect and retighten fasteners after assembly and during use.
  • Remove or replace the hanging toy if its cord or attachment frays.
  • Stop use if the post wobbles or structural material becomes exposed.

Maintenance burden

Vacuum fibers, inspect the base and post joint, retighten hardware, and check the attached toy before each use period.
