Tower of Tracks

The short answer: A contained, battery-free track toy for cats that enjoy batting and visual pursuit.

By Splintercat Editorial TeamNot hands-on testedSources checked August 30, 2026Methodology

Product evidence record

What this format is designed to do

Three stacked tracks keep the balls captive, which makes this a practical self-directed batting option during supervised use without loose balls rolling under furniture.

Play style
Independent batting
Size
10 × 10 × 5.25 in
Mechanism
3 tracks, 6 captive balls
Power
None
May suit

Cats that repeatedly bat at moving objects, supervised self-directed play, and compatible multi-cat play in a compact area.

Skip if

Your cat only engages with human-led prey movement, chews hard plastic, or becomes frustrated by toys whose ball cannot be captured.

Check before buying

  • Confirm the base grips your flooring instead of sliding during play.
  • Choose a placement with room for the cat to circle the toy.
  • Keep a human-led wand or toss toy in the rotation; this does not replace interactive play.

Safety and failure checks

  • Inspect the tracks and balls for cracks or separated parts before use.
  • Remove the toy if it becomes damaged or your cat attempts to chew pieces from it.
  • Introduce it in a quiet area and let the cat disengage freely.

Maintenance burden

Wipe the plastic and tracks with a damp cloth, dry fully, and clear trapped debris. Recheck the balls and track openings after cleaning.
