The short answer: A short multi-platform perch for homes prioritizing a small footprint and modest step height over maximum climbing height.

What this format is designed to do

At 22 inches high, this is better understood as a compact perch and scratching station than a full vertical-territory tower.

Nominal height
22 in
Nominal footprint
18 × 14 in
Listed fit
1–2 small/medium cats
Care
Spot clean
May suit

Kittens, small-to-medium cats, low windows, and cats suited to its lower step heights after individual mobility is confirmed.

Skip if

Your cat needs a high refuge, the listed 18-by-14-inch footprint is still too large, or carpeted surfaces conflict with the cat’s scratching preferences.

Check before buying

  • Measure the top platform, not only the total product size.
  • Compare step heights with the cat’s current mobility.
  • Place it where the cat gains a useful view without creating a fall route.

Safety and failure checks

  • Confirm the exact 22-inch variant and current stated weight guidance.
  • Inspect the jute, carpet, platforms, and assembly hardware regularly.
  • Do not treat a low perch as a complete substitute for other safe vertical territory.

Maintenance burden

Spot clean according to the current listing, vacuum hair and loose fibers, and retighten the assembly hardware as needed.
