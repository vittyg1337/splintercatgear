Ultimate Scratching Post

The short answer: A 32-inch woven-sisal post with a wide base for cats that prefer a full upright stretch.

What this format is designed to do

This format prioritizes usable vertical height, a broad base, and flat woven sisal instead of carpet.

Orientation
Vertical
Height
32 in
Base
16 × 16 in
Surface
Woven sisal
May suit

Adult cats that scratch vertically, reach high on furniture, and prefer a firm fibrous surface.

Skip if

Your cat primarily scratches floors or cardboard, the 16-inch square base blocks a walkway, or the post cannot sit near the cat’s chosen scratching location.

Check before buying

  • Measure the cat’s stretched reach and the full base footprint.
  • Place the post beside an existing scratch target or waking area.
  • Confirm assembly hardware is complete and can be retightened.

Safety and failure checks

  • Check the base and post fasteners regularly for movement.
  • Replace the post if structural material or fasteners become exposed.
  • Offer a separate horizontal option if the cat uses more than one orientation.

Maintenance burden

Vacuum shed fibers, inspect the top and base connections, and retighten the manufacturer-supplied hardware when necessary.
