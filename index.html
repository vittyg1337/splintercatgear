The Splintercat Standard

A recommendation should be traceable.

Every future product marked Hands-on Tested will show the conditions, measurements, drawbacks, and date tested.

Read the full method

Measure

Record dimensions, weight, capacity, noise, and other category-specific attributes.

Use

Observe assembly, daily operation, cleaning, and how the product fits into a real home.

Stress

Check stability and durability using safe, repeatable limits—not dramatic stunts.

Report

Publish strengths, failures, uncertainty, and the households that should skip it.

A legend leaves the woods

Splintercat exists to make gear decisions clearer, home setups smarter, and marketing language easier to challenge.
